## 1. Representative Task

Complete the Family Intake Form in the mother side of MotherCare prototype. Review the prefilled information, correct any the outdated phone number, describe priorities and submit the response, then update the profile to use the corrected number.

Starting State: Home Screen, with the default mother Jan Miller record and no submitted intake response. In this example she will change her phone number from 412-555-0104 to 412-555-0199 and enters that she needs to find food and childcare as her priority. Success is when the response is submitted and the answers and able to be reviews and the new phone number reflects the new correct one.

## 2. Hypothetical User

Jan is a mother preparing for her appointment. She uses her phone to do online forms. She knows her current household and contact information and has little time before her appointment.

## 3. Cognitive Walkthrough

### Find the form

Step 1: Find where to complete the form, Jan selects Forms in the bottom navigation on Home. There is visible text that matches the counselors request for her to complete the form. The Forms heading and cards then appear with a label at the top letting her know she's now on the forms view.

Step 2: Select the correct form: Select "Fill our form" on "Family Intake". The screen directs her to the correct form by highlighting in a darker green, with a badge that says start here. With a description of the form, and a time estimation (Fig 1). These cues distinguish intake from the other forms on the app. The intake form opens with pre populated profile information with a matching title name on screen so Jan knows she clicked the right form.

### Review and enter info

Step 3: Make sure prefilled details are accurate and change the now incorrect phone number is fixed. Jan leaves accurate information untouched. There is a panel explaining that your profile info was already filled out, but suggests doing a review incase anything has changed (Figure 2). Jan reviews them instead of thinking these are current, she then changes her phone number information.

Step 4: Jan fills our non prefilled information. She scrolls through the rest of the prefilled info and confirms its all correct. She then gets to the questions that are not prefilled. She sees the question about what help does her family currently need. She enters that they need help with food and childcare. This form goes beyond the initial screen and must be scrolled down on to fully interact. Every question is marked with required or optional labels that distinguish them. The answers remian visible in their field. 

### Submit Form

Step 5: Submit the form for completed responses. Select submit at the bottom of the form. The filled button follows the questions form terms. Submit and cancel buttons rest at the bottom. Jan must determine to hit the submit one. A phone change dialog opens instead of a success message. It explains that the phone number she entered has changed from her previous number. The earlier text box describing this warned that this popup could happen.

Step 6: Use the correct numbers for future forms. After Jan compares the old and new numbers she hits save in the dialog. This dialog asked whether to update the profile or not and shows both numbers together (figure 4). Jan must understand that save submits the response and updates profile number. When jan presses save is closes and displays the completion message. 

### Confirm completion

Step 7. Determine whether the task succeeded. Jan reads the completion message that states that you, your form is saved and confirms the response. A seperate sentence says the profile is updated and future forms will use the new details (Figure 5) Jan must notice both outcomes to understand that her form is complete. The screen the says her couselors name, and provides a Back to Froms, or edit response options.

Step 8. Return and see all other forms. Jan selects back to forms. The form she just filled out now states review submitted form rather than fill out form. Jan can recognize now that her form was submitted and done wihtout having to remember anything previously. THe review form reopens the saved answers, including the corrected number and priority. Its submit button has now changed to save changes rather than submit. 


### Alt Paths

1. You try to submit the form without all required questions answered. The form will move you back to the top most required unanswered questions. Jan is then directed to fill out the required question. 

2. In the phone number changed pop up dialog Jan selects No. The edited number is saved in the form response but the profile now keeps the old number. No is a valid choice with phone number changes and such, but it would not complete the task set out before hand where the phone number changes on the profile too.

3. If Jan edits a pre filled box such as phone number, then hits cancel, or leaves and reopens the form this box will be blank. The app goes back to forms without a discard warning, and any unsent change will get deleted. Jan could lose her work if she leaves unexpecdedly for any reason. 


## Analysis

1. Visibile Navigation and Groups

The labeled tabs at the bottom let Jan match what her goal is to an action without interpretation. In forms the position, color and Start Here text reinforce which form she is supposed to fill out (figure 1). The separate form ands distinction reduce the need to inspect every form you could fill out.

2. Prefilling reduces effort

The prefilling based on profile reduces cognitive load and repetitiveness, but you still have to review the information there. The explanation island explains this and invites the correction. The submit button and required questions are then on the bottom though. Required and optional tags help her distinguish what she has to fill out.

3. The confimation of changed pre filled info

The before and after comparison puts both old and new reducing the memory demand and helping Jan catch any mistakes. The decision still requires and understanding of what the no and save buttons do after you submit the form. No still keeps it changed in the form but doesnt change your profile. Save changes your profile and in the form.

## 5. Proposed Improvements

### Make the submission outcomes more clear

Keep the comparisions (Figure 4) but replace the current buttons with 3 different options. Submit and update profile, submit without updating profile, and back to form if it was a mistake.

### Confirm before unsent answers

When a change is unsubmitted and the mother goes back or changes navigation. Show a pop up that gives the option to Discard unsent answers? or Keep editing. Keep editing preserves the state before the leaving the form. Discard resets the form to before the mother filled anything. This changes the cancel option that was an issue.
