## 4. Related Work

### findhelp.org

FindHelp [1] is a website that connects people with resources based on a simple search and location. There is no login required, and when you search, it immediately pulls resources that are closest to you and most applicable to your search. It is a pretty simple result page that lists important information like location, hours of operation, and contact info.

This is similar to the vision for MotherCare in terms of the recommendation results and a simple interface. Both applications aim to connect users to real-world community resources (food, housing, childcare), and use location data to find the best resources near the user. Also, when you are signed in as a user, you have the ability to save resources to custom folders for future reference, which is similar to how MotherCare would build a profile for a mother over time.

However, FindHelp [1] mainly relies on search-driven results while MotherCare will have resources already loaded based on users profiles. While MotherCare should have a search feature, it should also use user information to automatically recommend resources based on their profile, without requiring a search at all. The main feature that would be used from this is the efficient and automatic resource recommender, because users of MotherCare may not have time to compare many different options, but just want a quick, good result.

### Microsoft Rewards

Microsoft Rewards [2] is part of microsoft that gives you incentives to interact with content (e.g. videos, articles, etc.). By interacting with this content users earn free rewards. The service has a dedicated page where you can see your points, what you earned them from as well as where to redeem your points.

Microsoft Rewards point redemption allows you to exchange your points for gift cards, subscriptions to microsoft products, or make donations to charity.

Mother Care would operate on a similar credit system gaining points for watching videos, reading articles, and staying consistent with your prescribed tasks from a councilor. This can tie into the self-help section of the app also for some of these tasks offering both self-help points and store points. 

Microsoft Rewards [2] targets mostly engagement, search engines and gaming. Mother Cares focus would be repuroposes towards health and community support for the Mothers. This encourages learning and user participation in the app while addressing the needs of the Mothers.

### 211

211 [3] is a service that helps connect people in need to relevant assistance. Users can narrow down the type of help they need to particular types of services such as caregiver resources or disaster recovery aid. These pages provide basic information and link to relevant resources such as the government benefits and financial help finder [4]. 211 also prominently displays options to call for assistance or find a local office across many of its pages.

Similarly, Mother Care will help people find resources that meet their needs. However, instead of primarily relying on users to identify and navigate to the type of assistance they need, Mother Care will collect initial information about a user's family in a check-in form and use that information to tailor resource recommendations on their profile. This funtionality is important for Mother Care since Family Life Network needs to understand a mother's overall situation, not simply point them to resources for individual issues. A mother may have pressing material needs while she is also interested in finding a local church to attend. Collecting all of this information allows Mother Care to provide ongoing support for a family rather than addressing a single problem. On the other hand, 211 is primarily a starting point for users to find services and organizations that can help them with specific issues.

### BetterHelp

BetterHelp [4] connects people with licensed therapists through an online platform. Its main purpose is mental-health support and counseling, rather than simply recommending self-help resources.
BetterHelp has an onboarding questionnaire that asks about things like goals, concerns, preferences, and what the user is looking for. That information is used to personalize the user's experience and match them with a therapist.
Mother Care [4] will likely be similar to BetterHelp because information from forms the users fill out could be saved and used to recommend resources, goals, priorities, etc.

### Bible App

The Bible App [5] is focuesed on sharing the Word of God through multiple mediums. The main function of the Bible App is naturally the words of the Bible, but also allows users to find local resources, such as churches, and web resources, such as ministry videos. 

MotherCare would benefit from encorperating a similar structure. The main focus of resources would be the provided physical locations with services such as food, clothing, and other meeting of physical needs. However, some resources that could be useful for mothers could be digital, such as videos or orgainizations that have an online presence instead of a brick-and-mortar front. MotherCare should especially attend to the seperation of the types of resources that the Bible App [x] employs. Physical and digital resources are both included in searches, but are easy to seperate before searching.

However, while the Bible App [5] prioritizes digital resources in their discover process, MotherCare would prioritize discovering physcial resources as it is created for a local ministry. The seperation but inclusion of digital resources with physical resources in the discover resources function of the Bible App will help meet the needs of the mothers in a wholistic but organized manor.
